How to Interact

  • Click the lamp to turn it on or off.
  • Move your mouse and a firefly follows you.
  • Hover over the cat to say hello.
  • Press 1, 2, 3 or 4 to change the sky (sunrise, day, sunset, night).
  • Press R, G or B to add a red, green or blue book. Press C to clear them.
  • Resize the window to switch to the compact layout.
  • Scroll to fill the progress bar at the top.
  • Reload the page: the lamp and sky are remembered.
...the city keeps its lights on...

About This Project

Night Room is a small interactive bedroom that responds to the user. It uses the DOM to change the HTML and CSS of the page, event listeners to react to the mouse, keyboard, and window, and the BOM to talk to the browser itself (window size, scrolling, localStorage, browser info, history, and opening new tabs).
